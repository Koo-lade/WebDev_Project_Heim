About Wheels on Fire!

Wheels on Fire was founded by our legendary CEO, John O'Reilly. O'Reilly has long held a passion for both business and cars, so what better way to harness his talents than to jump headfirst into the dealership business? For 10 years, O'Reilly and his highly adept staff has faithfully served customers, guiding them every step of the way to getting their dream ride. The story of John O'Reilly and Wheels on Fire is a testament to the success to be had when you take a leap, and we hope to help you take a leap into car ownership too.
